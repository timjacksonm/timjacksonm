Projects

TravelDot

A mobile pre-navigation safety app that aggregates real-time weather and road conditions into a simple route verdict (Clear / Caution / Hazard), so you can judge travel risk before starting navigation in your preferred maps app. I built it and shared it with my local community. React Native and Expo on the front end, with a TypeScript and Express API on Postgres.

Life Simulation

Here is a project I’ve kept alive over time: a full-stack simulation of Conway’s Game of Life. It’s simple, nerdy, and kind of hypnotic. Next.js and TypeScript on the front end, with an Express and MongoDB API.
